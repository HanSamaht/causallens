# Tagline Options

## Current (weak)
Ask Any Black-Box Model What Drives Its Decisions

## Option A — The Confession
Make any model confess.
它逼模型招供。审讯，不是解释。

## Option B — The Discovery
Your model is hiding something. Find out what.
你的模型在隐瞒什么。找出来。

## Option C — The Depth
Stop guessing why your model made that decision.
别猜模型为什么这么做。知道。

## Option D — The X-Ray
X-ray vision for machine learning.
机器学习的X光眼。

## Option E — The Interrogator
Interrogate any AI. Get causal answers, not correlations.
审讯任何AI。得到因果答案，不是相关。

## Option F — The Bold Claim
Every model has secrets. This finds them.
每个模型都有秘密。这个工具负责找出来。

## Option G — The Problem Finder
Find the hidden assumption that's ruining your model.
找出正在毁掉你模型的那个隐藏假设。

## Option H — The MiroFish Energy
Interrogate Anything.
审讯万物。

## My ranking:
1. **Option A** (Make any model confess) — short, aggressive, memorable, the interrogation metaphor is your differentiator
2. **Option H** (Interrogate Anything) — MiroFish-level viral energy, but defensible because "interrogate" is what you actually do
3. **Option B** (Your model is hiding something) — creates urgency, makes people click
4. **Option G** (Find the hidden assumption) — speaks directly to the backtest PF story
